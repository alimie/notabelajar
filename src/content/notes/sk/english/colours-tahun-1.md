## Colours / Warna

### Warna Asas (Primary Colours)

| English | Bahasa Melayu | Contoh |
|---------|----------------|--------|
| Red | Merah | 🍎 Apple is **red** |
| Blue | Biru | 🌊 Sea is **blue** |
| Yellow | Kuning | ☀️ Sun is **yellow** |

### Warna Lain

| English | Bahasa Melayu |
|---------|----------------|
| Green | Hijau |
| Orange | Oren |
| Purple | Ungu |
| Pink | Merah jambu |
| Black | Hitam |
| White | Putih |
| Brown | Perang / Coklat |
| Grey | Kelabu |

## Ayat Mudah

- The sky is **blue**. — Langit berwarna biru.
- I like **red** apples. — Saya suka epal merah.
- My bag is **black**. — Beg saya warna hitam.
- The grass is **green**. — Rumput itu hijau.

## Soalan & Jawapan

**What colour is it?** (Apakah warnanya?)

- **What colour is the banana?** — It is **yellow**.
- **What colour is your shirt?** — It is **blue**.

> **Tips:** Cuba tanya "What colour is this?" bila nampak benda sekeliling. Seronok dan cepat belajar!
